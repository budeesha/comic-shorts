Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026

Comic Shorts Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool run by the owner of the For Fun YouTube channel to upload and schedule that channel's own comic-strip videos. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it's used and stored, and how to remove access.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app asks for two permissions:

  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.upload): to upload videos and set their title, description, tags, category, audience setting and scheduled publish time.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to confirm which channel is connected and to check the processing and scheduling status of videos the app uploaded.

The app doesn't access viewers' data, comments, subscribers, analytics, payments, or any other Google service.

How the data is used

  • Only to upload and schedule the channel owner's own videos on the owner's own channel.
  • It's never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

How the data is stored

  • The Google sign-in token is stored only in a local file on the channel owner's computer. It isn't sent to any server other than Google's.
  • The app keeps a local log of uploaded video IDs and their scheduled times. It doesn't store data about other people.
  • The app has no servers, databases, cookies or analytics.

Keeping and deleting data

Local files are kept only while the app is in use. Deleting the app's folder removes all locally stored data, including the sign-in token. YouTube API data obtained by the app is refreshed or deleted within 30 days, in line with the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

Removing access

You can remove the app's access to your Google account at any time from the Google security settings page (Third-party apps & services → Comic Shorts Uploader → Remove access).

Children

The app is not directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
