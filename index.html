Turning our comic strips into YouTube Shorts

Comic Shorts Uploader is a small, private tool used by the creator of the For Fun YouTube channel to publish their own comic strips as Shorts.

What it does

  • Turns each original comic strip into a vertical video (1080×1920) with a short opening card and a panel-by-panel reveal.
  • Uploads the finished video to the creator's own For Fun channel as private, with a scheduled publish time, title, description and tags.
  • Runs once a day on the creator's own computer, so new comics go out on a regular schedule.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. It isn't a public service: there's no sign-up, and it works only with the owner's own channel and content. It signs in through Google's standard OAuth consent screen and uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos.

Your data

The tool keeps nothing beyond what it needs to upload videos to the owner's channel. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
